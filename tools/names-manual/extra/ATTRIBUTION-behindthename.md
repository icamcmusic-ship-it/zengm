# Attribution: behindthename.com

The name lists in `btn/` (`tools/names-manual/extra/btn/country-*-first.csv` and `-last.csv`) were compiled from the
given-name and surname databases of [Behind the Name](https://www.behindthename.com/) (behindthename.com) and its
sister site [Behind the Surname](https://surnames.behindthename.com/), by Mongoose Technologies.

The data is licensed under [Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International (CC BY-SA 4.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).
Changes: names were assigned to countries, filtered (female names, feminine surname forms, fictional or historical
names, obscure variants), normalized (accents removed where the existing data for that country has none) and given
frequency weights; only the names and these derived weights are included.

Because of the ShareAlike term, the derived files in `btn/` (and the combined `data/names.json`) are shared under the same license, CC BY-SA 4.0.
