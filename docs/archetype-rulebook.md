# Archetype judging rulebook

archrules. arch17 found two judging passes of the archetype answer key share a build for only 55.5% of players, so the judged key
was inconsistent. This rulebook is how every answer key is judged from now on: explicit, measurable criteria per word of a build
name, a precedence rule, and a fallback. It is also exactly what a build's name promises (the name-promise checks read it), so the
judge and the promises cannot drift apart.

- Machine-readable: `src/common/archetypes/rulebook.data.ts` (`RULES`, `WORD_RULES` and the constants below).
- The judge: `src/common/archetypes/rulebook.ts` `ruleJudge(builds, { cv, style })`, deterministic, 1-3 builds best first.
- The rule answer key: `src/common/archetypes/ruleKey.data.ts` (`RULE_KEY`, by judged player key), regenerated by
  `RUN_ARCHETYPE_RULEKEY=1 npx vitest --run src/common/archetypes/ruleKey.script.test.ts` (`ARCH_RULEKEY_OUT=file` for the report,
  `ARCH_RULEKEY_DRY=1` report only, `ARCH_RULEKEY_QUANTILES=1` regenerates the percentile table). Latest report:
  `tools/archrules/rulekey-report.txt`.
- Tests: `src/common/archetypes/rulebook.test.ts`.

## Scales

- **Blended class reading** (`cv`, `score.ts classValues`): per skill class (shoot = three-point, mid, finish, post, handle,
  pass, reb, rimD = rim protection / interior D, perD = perimeter D / steals, ath; plus stre, def = either side, score = any
  shot) his standing at his position blended with the shape of his own game, his stats and (Sub-Ratings on) his sub-rating leans.
  Judging bars are **"top X%"** of players on it, read through `ruleQuantiles.data.ts` (its percentiles over the 549 judged
  players: real NBA 1992-2025, a random league, draft prospects, a simulated league).
- **Sub-rating leans** (`sub`, `subStyle.ts subFeatures`, z against the league's own table): checked only with Sub-Ratings on.
  blk = shot blocking, idf = interior D, pdf = perimeter D, stl = steals, rng = range, cs = catch-and-shoot vs off the dribble,
  pst = post moves, mxv = max vertical vs standing, orb = offensive vs defensive boards, vis = vision.
- **Play types** (`pt`, share of his possessions against his position's peers, z): checked only with Advanced Tracking Stats
  and a real sample (weight possessions / (possessions + half) at least 0.3). One of the listed play types is enough.
- **Promise floor**: every class a name promises must be at least 0 z (ordinary for him) for every holder (`FLOOR`,
  `score.ts PROMISE_MET`); the Sniper words raise it to 0.8 z on the three.

## Rules per word

A build's words are its name's concepts (`twoK/concepts.ts`: the regexes there decide which words are which concept). The
**head** is the last word that is not a modifier ("Sniper" in "Crafty 3PT Sniper"; "Scorer" is the head only when nothing else
is named). The head word's bar is **lead** (for a word with two classes, one of them at lead and the other at support); every other
word's bar is **support**. "none" means no bar beyond the promise floor.

**archuser2k words** (the user's own NBA 2K archetypes, `user2k.data.ts`): synonyms of an existing word joined its concept
(Pesky / Pest / Bandit: steals; Tip-In / Possession Extender: the offensive glass; Bouncy / Athlete / Apex: above the rim;
Bruiser / Bruising / Punishing / Punisher: strength; Facilitating / General / Dish: passing; Hash-Sinking: the mid-range;
Bomber: threes; Tenacious / Warden: the perimeter; Paint Defender / Paint Protector / Paint-Protecting / Terror / Shot-Axing /
Fortress / Pillar: the rim (and no longer the Paint or Defender words); Break Leader: outlets; Splash: spacing; Downhill:
slashing; Levitator: finishing; Slammer / Flusher: dunks; Dribble Tech / Off-the-Bounce: creating; Floor Sweeper:
rebounding; Finesse / Dynamic: style; High-Post is not a Post word). The new words have their own rules: **Burner / Open-Lane**
(athleticism, transition share), **Hammer** (finishing and athleticism, max-vertical lean), **Cutter / Off-Ball**
(athleticism, cut share), **Scooper** (finishing, close-shot lean), **Pull-Up** (mid-range, shot-creation lean), **Chaser /
Bucket Chaser** (mid-range and three), **Grab & Go** (rebounding), **Pressure Cooker** (perimeter D, perimeter D lean
+0.25 z), **Wizard** (passing and handling, vision lean) and **High-Low** (post, post-moves lean). Adding them changed no
existing build's words, promises or wants (checked against a snapshot of every build and every 2K name).

**Whole-name words** (`WORD_RULES`):

- **Sniper** (Sniper, Sharpshooter, Laser, Automatic, Logo, Big Range, Silk): three-point skill **top 15%** AND range sub
  (rng) +0.84 z (about top 20%). Promise floor raised to 0.8 z on the three.
- **High-Flying** (High-Flying, Aerial, Glide, Athletic, Athlete, Explosive, Force of Nature, Bouncy, Apex): athleticism **top 25%** AND max vertical
  lean (mxv) +0.25 z. (The reading also checks the leaping itself: `score.ts absoluteParts` air.)

Examples in words: **Rim Protector / Shot-Erasing / Eraser** as the head: rim protection top 20% and (Sub-Ratings) blocks lean
+0.52 z. **Lob Threat**: finishing top 25%, max vertical lean +0.25 z, and (play types) roll man or cutter share +0.25 z.
**Lockdown**: perimeter D top 20% and perimeter D lean +0.25 z. **Ball-Hawking / Pickpocket**: perimeter D top 20% and steals
lean +0.52 z. **Playmaker / Dime / Diming / Point Forward**: passing top 25%, vision lean 0 or better, P&R ball handler share 0
or better. **Post / Bully**: post top 25% (+ post-moves lean), strength top 30% when Bully is the head. **Glass / Board /
Cleaner**: rebounding top 25%. **Stretch / Pick & Pop**: three top 25% (spot-up or off-screen share 0 or better).
**Mid-Range / Middy**: mid-range top 25%. **3-Level / Versatile Scorer**: three and mid-range both top 40%, one top 30%.
**2-Way**: defense on either side top 30% as the head, top 40% as a modifier. **Crafty, Skilled, Versatile, Fluid, Riser**: style
words, they promise and require nothing.

## Qualifying, precedence and fallback

A build **qualifies** for a player when (1) his position allows it, (2) every word's bar is met (the classes, the sub-ratings
when present, the play types when present), (3) the head word's class is one of his **three best skill classes** (by percentile:
what a build is first of all must be what he is), and (4) for a **generic** build (`generic.ts GENERIC_BUILDS`) or an all-around
one, he is genuinely all-around: at least 4 of the 10 skill classes in the top 40%.

**Precedence** when several qualify: (1) specific before generic; (2) coverage: the build whose words name more of his three best
skill classes; (3) family priority: the build whose head word is his best skill first; (4) specificity: more measurable words;
(5) the larger margin above the bars; (6) id. The first three are his acceptable builds.

**None qualifies**: the nearest specific build (smallest total shortfall in z below the bars), plus any within 0.25 z of it, at
most three. Never a generic build unless it qualifies.

## Who uses it

- **Answer keys**: `ruleKey.data.ts` is the rule key over the judged population; `fit.script.test.ts` fits or scores against it
  with `ARCH_KEY=rule`.
- **Name promises**: `score.ts promisesOf` (and so `promiseChecks`, `promiseMiss`, the promise penalties and the name-promise
  report `namePromise.script.test.ts`) read each word's classes, the promise floor and the Sniper / High-Flying words from
  `rulebook.data.ts`. `rulebook.test.ts` checks that every word of the build vocabulary has a rule and that each build's
  promised classes are exactly the classes the rulebook requires of it.
- **User picks (archfb)**: the developer refit (`ARCH_PICKS=...`) uses the user's picks as the answer key. Where a user has
  picked a build for a player, the pick overrides the rule-key label; the rule key is the reference everywhere else.

## Changing the rules

Change `rulebook.data.ts` (and this page), rerun `ruleKey.script.test.ts` to regenerate the key and its report, run
`rulebook.test.ts`. Changing a word's **classes** changes what names promise and so the shipped reading: rerun the archetype
metrics and the outcome-invariance test before shipping. Bars, sub-rating and play-type criteria only change the judged key.
